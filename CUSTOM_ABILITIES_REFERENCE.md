# Custom Abilities - Quick Reference

## 🎮 Player Cheat Sheet

This reference card provides quick lookups for all 18 custom abilities introduced in the Custom Racing Cards set.

---

## Ability Icons Legend

- ⚔️ **Offensive** - Disrupt or damage opponents
- 🛡️ **Defensive** - Protect yourself or recover
- ⚡ **High-Risk** - Powerful but with drawbacks
- 🎯 **Positional** - Requires specific positioning
- 🔄 **Synergy** - Combos with other cards
- 🧠 **Strategic** - Planning and information
- 💨 **Area Control** - Affects track zones
- 🏁 **Comeback** - Better when behind

---

## Abilities A-Z

### ADRENALINE 🏁
**Cost Value:** 3
**Effect:** For each car you're behind, gain +1 movement (max +3)
**When to Use:** Best when in last place; weak when leading
**Combos With:** High-movement cards for massive catch-up

---

### BACKUP PLAN 🧠
**Cost Value:** 2
**Effect:** If this is the last card in your hand, you may play it twice
**When to Use:** Save for critical moments; plan hand order carefully
**Combos With:** Card draw effects to control hand size

---

### BUMPER 🎯
**Cost Value:** 2.5
**Effect:** If you end adjacent to another car, push them 1 segment
**When to Use:** Tactical positioning near hazards or corners
**Combos With:** High-movement cards to ensure adjacency

---

### DRIFT 🔄
**Cost Value:** 3
**Effect:** Gain 1 momentum of LEFT or RIGHT (your choice) after playing
**When to Use:** Fix momentum mismatches; enable turn chains
**Combos With:** Turn-heavy tracks; other momentum cards

---

### EXHAUST TRAIL 💨
**Cost Value:** 2.5
**Effect:** Leave a cloud that slows cars by -1 movement (lasts 2 turns)
**When to Use:** Leaders slowing followers; zone control
**Warning:** Can affect you on lap races

---

### MIRROR 🧠
**Cost Value:** 4
**Effect:** Copy the ability of the last card played by any opponent
**When to Use:** Reactive play against strong opponent abilities
**Tip:** Track what opponents play; useless if they play basic cards

---

### MOMENTUM SHIFT 🔄
**Cost Value:** 2
**Effect:** Change this card's momentum to any type when revealed
**When to Use:** Ultimate flexibility; never a dead draw
**Combos With:** Complex tracks with varied turn requirements

---

### OVERDRIVE ⚡
**Cost Value:** 3
**Effect:** Double this card's movement, take 1 unavoidable damage
**When to Use:** Critical positioning moments; final lap sprints
**Warning:** Multiple uses = multiple damage; use sparingly

---

### PIT STOP 🧠
**Cost Value:** 4
**Effect:** FORGET 1 card, DRAW 2 next turn, gain +1 XP
**When to Use:** Deck refinement; remove weak cards
**Value:** Net +1 card advantage plus deck thinning

---

### PRECISION 🎯
**Cost Value:** 2.5
**Effect:** Ignore all obstacles and hazards on the track
**When to Use:** Hazard-heavy tracks; against oil/obstacle strategies
**Value:** Increases as hazards accumulate

---

### RELAY 🔄
**Cost Value:** 3
**Effect:** If played adjacent in sequence, both cards gain +1 movement
**When to Use:** Sequencing matters; planning phase
**Combos With:** Multiple RELAY cards for chain bonuses; BOOST

---

### REPAIR 🛡️
**Cost Value:** 3
**Effect:** Remove 1 negative effect OR return 1 forgotten card to deck
**When to Use:** Counter hooks/oil; recover deck in long games
**Flexibility:** Choose effect based on situation

---

### SABOTAGE ⚔️
**Cost Value:** 2.5
**Effect:** Target opponent's next card has -1 movement (min 0)
**When to Use:** Disrupt leader; slow down key turns
**Limitations:** Less effective vs multi-movement or WILD cards

---

### SCOUT 🧠
**Cost Value:** 2
**Effect:** Look at next 3 track segments and gain +1 XP
**When to Use:** Unknown tracks; optimize card sequencing
**Bonus:** Always gets +1 XP even if info not useful

---

### SHORTCUT 🎯
**Cost Value:** 3
**Effect:** Move through 1 obstacle or off-track without penalty
**When to Use:** Alternative pathing; risky position gains
**Combos With:** High-movement cards for aggressive lines

---

### SLIPSTREAM 🎯
**Cost Value:** 4
**Effect:** Follow behind a car within 4 segments, copy their next movement
**When to Use:** Trailing fast opponents; positioning behind leaders
**Limitation:** Requires being behind; useless in first place

---

### TAILGATE 🎯
**Cost Value:** 3
**Effect:** If directly behind another car, gain SHIELD(1) and +1 movement
**When to Use:** Aggressive following; packed races
**Requirement:** Must be directly behind (not just within range)

---

### TURBO BOOST ⚡
**Cost Value:** 2
**Effect:** Gain +2 movement this turn, then FORGET this card
**When to Use:** One-time power spike; critical turns only
**Warning:** Permanent loss; cannot be recovered with REPAIR

## Combos & Synergies

### 🔥 **Power Combos**

**RELAY + RELAY**
- Both cards in sequence = both get +1 movement
- 3+ RELAY cards = massive movement chain

**SLIPSTREAM + TAILGATE**
- Follow strong opponent, copy their movement
- Gain SHIELD(1) and +1 extra movement

**DRIFT + MOMENTUM SHIFT**
- Ultimate momentum flexibility
- Never have wrong momentum type

**PIT STOP + REPAIR**
- Forget weak card with Pit Stop
- Recover strong forgotten card with Repair
- Deck optimization combo

**TURBO BOOST + OVERDRIVE**
- Nitrous card: +2 from Turbo, double with Overdrive
- Extreme movement spike (4+ movement from single card)

**SCOUT + MOMENTUM SHIFT**
- Scout ahead to see track
- Shift momentum to match upcoming turns

**MIRROR + High-Value Opponents**
- Copy opponent's GRAPPLE, CHAINLINK, or OFFLOAD
- Get premium abilities at standard cost

**ADRENALINE + SHORTCUT**
- Massive comeback movement from last place
- Shortcut through obstacles for risky catch-up

## Counter-Strategies

### Countering Aggressive Players
- **Use:** PRECISION (ignore their hazards), REPAIR (remove their effects)
- **Buy:** SHIELD, STEADFAST for protection
- **Avoid:** Bunching up (limits BUMPER, TAILGATE)

### Countering Slipstream/Tailgate
- **Strategy:** Don't stay in first place long
- **Use:** EXHAUST TRAIL to punish followers
- **Buy:** Cards without strong abilities (nothing to copy)

### Countering Deck Manipulation
- **Strategy:** Aggressive early game before they refine deck
- **Use:** SABOTAGE their key turns
- **Avoid:** Long games where refined decks dominate

### Countering Comeback Mechanics
- **Strategy:** Maintain mid-pack position
- **Use:** SABOTAGE when they're behind
- **Avoid:** Creating huge leads (activates ADRENALINE max)

## Common Mistakes

### ❌ **Turbo Boost Too Early**
Using TURBO BOOST on turn 2-3 wastes the one-time power spike
✅ **Instead:** Save for final lap or critical positioning moments

### ❌ **Overdrive Spam**
Multiple OVERDRIVE uses = multiple damage, can cost you the race
✅ **Instead:** Use 1-2 times maximum, only when necessary

### ❌ **Mirror with No Targets**
Buying MIRROR when opponents play basic cards = wasted purchase
✅ **Instead:** Buy MIRROR when opponents have strong abilities

### ❌ **Slipstream in First**
SLIPSTREAM is useless when leading
✅ **Instead:** Avoid buying SLIPSTREAM if you tend to lead

### ❌ **Pit Stop Too Late**
Using PIT STOP on final lap gets no value from deck refinement
✅ **Instead:** Use PIT STOP early-mid game for maximum benefit

### ❌ **Exhaust Your Own Path**
Leaving EXHAUST TRAIL on lap-style tracks can hurt you next lap
✅ **Instead:** Use EXHAUST on point-to-point races or to block side paths

### ❌ **Adrenaline While Leading**
ADRENALINE gives +0 movement when in first = terrible value
✅ **Instead:** Only buy ADRENALINE for comeback decks

### ❌ **Backup Plan Misplay**
Playing BACKUP PLAN early in hand wastes the double-use potential
✅ **Instead:** Plan hand order to make it last card

## FAQs

**Q: Can MIRROR copy another MIRROR?**
A: Yes, but the second MIRROR would then copy the ability that the first MIRROR copied (not MIRROR itself).

**Q: Does OVERDRIVE double momentum too?**
A: Yes, it doubles ALL movement from the card, including momentum.

**Q: Can REPAIR recover TURBO BOOST cards?**
A: No, TURBO BOOST uses FORGET (permanent removal), REPAIR can't return permanently forgotten cards.

**Q: Does RELAY work with BOOST?**
A: They stack! RELAY affects the card itself, BOOST affects adjacent cards.

**Q: What if ADRENALINE ties for position?**
A: Count cars with equal or better position as "ahead of you."

**Q: Can SHORTCUT go completely off-track?**
A: Yes, but only through 1 off-track space. You can't traverse multiple off-track spaces.

**Q: Does TAILGATE require exact space or just behind?**
A: Directly behind means 0 spaces between you and the car ahead.

**Q: Can SLIPSTREAM copy WILD movement?**
A: Yes, you copy their actual movement choice, not just the symbol.

**Q: Does MOMENTUM SHIFT change the card permanently?**
A: No, it's a one-time choice when revealed. Next time you draw it, choose again.

**Q: Can EXHAUST TRAIL stack?**
A: Multiple clouds on same space stack (-2 movement for 2 clouds, etc.).
