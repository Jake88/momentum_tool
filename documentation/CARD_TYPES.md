# Card types

- Drive cards
- Power cards
- Drivers cards
- Car cards
- Car upgrade cards

## Drive cards
Anatomy of a drive card:
- Set: 'Basic' || 'Advanced'
- Type: Acceleration || Top speed || Handling || Decisive
- Momentum: MovementSymbol
- Movement: MovementSymbol[]
- Combo: MovementSymbol // Left most symbol in the movement row could be a 'combo', where it has a backgroud colour and label that requires the previous card to match that color / type to be eligible 
- Conserved Fuel ??

## Power cards
Anatomy of a drive card:
- Set: 'Power'
- Type: 'Power'
- Bronze movement: MovementSymbol (Available for anyone coming 3rd or worse)
- Silver movement: MovementSymbol (Available for anyone coming 2nd or worse)
- Gold movement: MovementSymbol (Available for anyone coming 1st or worse)
- Ability: Ability
