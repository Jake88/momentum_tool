# Experimental Magic Schools - Unconventional Concepts

**Purpose**: Explore bizarre, unconventional, and rarely-seen magic types that break away from traditional fantasy tropes. These concepts can supplement or replace existing schools for unique gameplay experiences.

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

### Criteria for Experimental Magic
1. **Uncommon Concepts**: Avoid overused fantasy magic (fire, ice, lightning are baseline)
2. **Color Innovation**: Use unexpected color/concept pairings
3. **Mechanical Uniqueness**: Each school should offer gameplay never seen before
4. **Thematic Depth**: Concept should support 30+ card designs
5. **Balance Potential**: Must fit within existing cost system

## 1. 🟡 GOLD - Luck & Probability Magic

### **Concept**: Manipulation of chance, fortune, and probability
**Wizard**: Lucky Gambler / Fortune's Fool

### **Core Mechanics**
- Dice rolls, coin flips, random outcomes with weighted probabilities
- "Re-roll" abilities (reroll movement, reroll opponent's spell)
- Probability manipulation (increase crit chance, force failures)
- High variance: Cards can be amazing or terrible depending on luck

### **Sample Abilities**
- **FORTUNE'S FAVOR**: Flip coin - Heads: +4 movement, Tails: -1 movement
- **LOADED DICE**: Roll d6, move that many segments in any direction
- **PROBABILITY SHIFT**: Force opponent to re-roll their next spell
- **LUCKY BREAK**: Ignore next negative effect (50% chance)
- **GAMBLER'S FALLACY**: Each failed spell this turn makes next spell stronger
- **RIGGED ODDS**: Next 3 coin flips are Heads
- **CHAOS CASCADE**: Chain random effects (d6: 1-2 damage, 3-4 teleport, 5-6 heal)

### **Mount Synergy**
- **Chaos Rabbit** (Ground) - Passive: Coin flips are always 60/40 in your favor
- **Fortune Drake** (Flying) - Passive: Re-roll one dice per turn

### **Playstyle**: High-risk gamblers, swingy outcomes, "going all-in" strategies

## 2. 🔶 ORANGE - Rust & Entropy Magic

### **Concept**: Decay, degradation, corrosion, and deterioration
**Wizard**: Rust Sage / Entropy Cultist

### **Core Mechanics**
- Permanent degradation of opponent resources
- "Rust" counters that accumulate and weaken
- Destroying equipment, obstacles dissolve over time
- Anti-magic (degrades spells, reduces costs to zero)

### **Sample Abilities**
- **RUST TOUCH**: Target opponent's spells cost +1 permanently this race
- **ENTROPY WAVE**: All obstacles on track crumble and disappear
- **DEGRADATION**: Each turn, opponent loses 1 max HP (irreversible)
- **CORROSION**: Opponent's mount loses passive ability for 3 turns
- **DECAY AURA**: Adjacent opponents' cards lose 1 movement permanently
- **OXIDIZE**: Destroy target enchantment or buff
- **TIME'S TOLL**: Age the track - create hazards that worsen each lap

### **Mount Synergy**
- **Rust Elemental** (Ground/Flying) - Passive: Leave corrosion trail (reduces movement by 1)
- **Decay Phoenix** (Flying) - Passive: Resurrects weaker each time (loses 1 HP per rebirth)

### **Playstyle**: Attrition, permanent resource denial, anti-buff

## 💡 Implementation Notes

### For Each School, Define:
1. **15-20 Abilities** (VALUE 2-5 range)
2. **30-40 Spell Cards** (Common through Legendary)
3. **3-5 Mount Options** (with school-specific passives)
4. **Wizard Definition** (rider ability, deck bonuses)
5. **Synergies/Rivalries** (with other schools)

### Balance Considerations:
- High variance schools (Luck, Flux) should have lower average power
- Permanent effects (Rust, Age, Blood Pacts) must cost more
- Existence manipulation (Void) needs heavy restrictions
- HP-as-resource (Blood) needs careful HP scaling
