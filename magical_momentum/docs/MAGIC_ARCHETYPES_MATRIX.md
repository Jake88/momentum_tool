# Magic School Archetypes - Gameplay Matrix System

**Purpose**: Systematically categorize all magic schools by gameplay dimensions to enable diverse playstyle customization. Players can select schools matching their preferred racing experience (high chaos vs low chaos, aggressive vs defensive, etc.).

---

## 🎯 Gameplay Dimensions (6 Metrics)

Each magic school is rated 1-5 on these dimensions:

### **1. SPEED** ⚡
Raw velocity and movement bonuses
- **1**: Slow, methodical
- **3**: Average racing pace
- **5**: Blazing fast, speed-focused

### **2. CHAOS** 🎲
Randomness, variance, unpredictability
- **1**: Consistent, reliable
- **3**: Some variance
- **5**: Wild, random outcomes

### **3. MOBILITY** 🔄
Cornering, positioning, maneuverability
- **1**: Straight-line only
- **3**: Moderate turning
- **5**: Extreme agility, teleportation

### **4. DEFENSE** 🛡️
Protection, evasion, damage prevention
- **1**: Glass cannon
- **3**: Some protection
- **5**: Fortress, nearly invincible

### **5. AGGRESSION** ⚔️
Direct opponent disruption and damage
- **1**: Passive, non-interactive
- **3**: Moderate interaction
- **5**: Maximum disruption

### **6. CONTROL** 🎮
Board manipulation, opponent restrictions
- **1**: No opponent control
- **3**: Some positioning control
- **5**: Total lockdown

## 🔍 IDENTIFIED GAPS IN DESIGN SPACE

After analyzing all 28 schools, these archetype combinations are MISSING:

### **HIGH SPEED + LOW CHAOS** (Reliable Speed Demons)
Current highest: Storm (5 speed, 2 chaos) - but only one school!
**Need**: More consistent speed options

### **HIGH MOBILITY + HIGH DEFENSE** (Evasive Tanks)
Current: Only Nature (4/4) and Malleability (4/4)
**Need**: More evasive defensive options

### **HIGH AGGRESSION + HIGH DEFENSE** (Bruisers)
Current: NONE! Gap in design space
**Need**: Tanky aggressive options

### **LOW CHAOS + HIGH CONTROL** (Precision Controllers)
Current: Ice (1 chaos, 5 control), Stasis (1/5)
**Need**: More reliable control options

### **EXTREME SPECIALISTS** (5/5 in one stat, 1/1 in others)
Current: Few extreme specialists
**Need**: Glass cannon archetypes

### **BALANCED GENERALISTS** (3 in everything)
Current: Several close, but none perfect
**Need**: True all-rounder options

## 1. 🔵 SAPPHIRE - Velocity & Acceleration Magic

**Archetype**: HIGH SPEED + LOW CHAOS (Reliable Speed)
**Ratings**: Speed 5, Chaos 1, Mobility 3, Defense 2, Aggression 2, Control 2

### **Concept**: Pure velocity without randomness
**Wizard**: Velocity Sage

### **Core Mechanics**
- Acceleration stacks (gain speed over time)
- Consistent speed bonuses (no RNG)
- Slipstream mechanics (draft behind opponents)
- Terminal velocity (cap at maximum speed)

### **Sample Abilities**
- **ACCELERATE**: Gain +1 movement each turn (stacks to +3)
- **TERMINAL VELOCITY**: Set your movement to exactly 5 (no variance)
- **SLIPSTREAM**: +2 movement when directly behind opponent
- **SONIC CRUISE**: Maintain last turn's movement speed
- **MOMENTUM**: Each STRAIGHT movement +1 to next STRAIGHT
- **OVERDRIVE**: +4 movement, cannot exceed this amount
- **SPEED LOCK**: Set speed to 4, cannot be changed for 2 turns

### **Mount Options**
- **Velocity Drake** - Passive: +1 speed per consecutive turn racing
- **Sonic Pegasus** - Passive: Never slowed below 3 movement

**Playstyle**: Consistent high-speed racing, no surprises, pure velocity

## 2. 🟣 VIOLET - Barrier & Ward Magic

**Archetype**: HIGH DEFENSE + HIGH CONTROL (Defensive Controller)
**Ratings**: Speed 2, Chaos 1, Mobility 2, Defense 5, Aggression 2, Control 5

### **Concept**: Impenetrable defenses that restrict movement
**Wizard**: Ward Keeper

### **Core Mechanics**
- Layered shields (multiple defense layers)
- Ward zones (protected areas)
- Barrier placement (block paths defensively)
- Sanctuary (safe zones)

### **Sample Abilities**
- **LAYERED WARD**: Gain 3 shield layers (1 damage removes 1 layer)
- **SANCTUARY**: Create safe zone (no damage taken inside for 2 turns)
- **BARRIER WALL**: Place 3 barriers in defensive positions
- **AEGIS**: Become immune to next 2 attacks
- **WARD FIELD**: 3x3 area grants +2 defense to all allies
- **FORTRESS**: Cannot move, gain 5 shields
- **REFLECT WARD**: Damage dealt to you damages attacker equally

### **Mount Options**
- **Ward Dragon** - Passive: Start with 2 shield layers
- **Barrier Golem** - Passive: Obstacles near you grant +1 defense

**Playstyle**: Extreme defense, lock down areas, survive everything

## 3. ⚫ COAL - Inertia & Momentum Physics

**Archetype**: HIGH MOBILITY + HIGH DEFENSE (Evasive Tank)
**Ratings**: Speed 3, Chaos 1, Mobility 5, Defense 5, Aggression 1, Control 2

### **Concept**: Physics-based defense through momentum conservation
**Wizard**: Inertia Master

### **Core Mechanics**
- Momentum conservation (maintain velocity)
- Inertial dampening (reduce impacts)
- Elastic collisions (bounce off obstacles)
- Angular momentum (turning maintains speed)

### **Sample Abilities**
- **CONSERVATION**: Movement from last turn carries to this turn
- **DAMPENING**: Take 50% damage from all sources (physics absorption)
- **ELASTIC BOUNCE**: Hitting obstacle bounces you forward instead
- **ANGULAR TWIST**: Turning doesn't reduce speed
- **MOMENTUM SHIELD**: Gain shield equal to your movement speed
- **FRICTIONLESS**: Ignore all terrain penalties, maintain momentum
- **IMPACT REDIRECT**: When hit, move 2 segments in any direction

### **Mount Options**
- **Inertia Elemental** - Passive: Cannot be stopped (immune to stuns)
- **Momentum Wyrm** - Passive: +1 defense for each segment moved

**Playstyle**: Unstoppable momentum, evade through motion, physics tank
